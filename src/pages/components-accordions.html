How are invoices sent to customers?
Invoices are created when an order is paid and sent by email the same minute. Customers can download every invoice again from their account page. For B2B customers the invoice also includes the VAT number.
Can a customer change the delivery address?
Yes, until the order is packed. After that, the customer contacts support and the warehouse changes the label. The order page shows the current status in real time.
Who can issue a refund?
Owners and administrators can refund any order. Editors can refund up to $200; larger amounts need an approval, which goes to the finance team by email.
Compact accordion with a gap
